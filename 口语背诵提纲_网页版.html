01

飞盘

Frisbee

可套用题型 TOPIC LINKS

  • Participating in team activities
  • Matches
  • Hobbies
  • Sports

参考正文 SAMPLE ANSWER

My favourite free-time activity is playing frisbee with friends.

When I first tried it, I was terrible at throwing and catching. I simply copied my friends’ movements, and they patiently walked me through the basic skills.

I kept practising and made huge progress. Later, I joined the school frisbee team. Whenever I felt confused about the game, our coach and captain gave me plenty of useful advice on game tactics and teamwork.

Playing frisbee has taught me two valuable lessons. First, teamwork is the foundation of victory—no one can win a game alone. Second, it built up my resilience. Even when I dropped passes or lost matches, I refused to give up.

Overall, frisbee is super rewarding. It brings me happiness, lifelong friends and meaningful life lessons.

考场速记 QUICK NOTES

  1. 01free-time hobby
  2. 02first try: bad at throwing/catching → copy friends → learn the basics
  3. 03practise → make progress → join the school team
  4. 04coach & captain: tactics + teamwork
  5. 05lessons: teamwork + never give up
  6. 06feeling: rewarding, fun and friendship

I’d like to talk about my dream job: working as a short-term frisbee coach overseas.

This is my big goal, so I practise frisbee and English every single day to get ready for it.

I am drawn to the job because I can meet people from different countries and share my passion for frisbee. Working outdoors also keeps me healthy, so I run every day to stay fit.

On top of that, I hope to hone my cross-cultural communication skills. For me, this job would allow me to adapt to life overseas and make new friends while doing something I genuinely love.

03

人物

My Frisbee Coach

可套用题型 TOPIC LINKS

  • A person you admire
  • A difficult thing
  • A change in your life
  • Good news
  • Advice
  • Adapting to something new

参考正文 SAMPLE ANSWER

I want to talk about my frisbee coach, who helped me improve a lot.

When I first joined the school frisbee team, I was awful. I couldn’t throw or catch well and knew nothing about game tactics. I messed up in matches and almost quit.

My coach saw my problems. He stayed after training to practise with me, taught me basic skills and explained game strategies. He kept encouraging me and told me that frisbee relies on teamwork.

I took his advice and trained hard. Soon, I played better and joined a school competition.

I truly admire him. He taught me to be persistent and work with others. I would never have improved without his help.

考场速记 QUICK NOTES

  1. 01intro: frisbee coach + school team
  2. 02problems: poor skills + no tactics + almost quit
  3. 03help: extra training + skills + encouragement + teamwork
  4. 04progress: follow advice → train hard → enter competition
  5. 05feeling: admiration + persistence + teamwork

04

喜欢的学科

Science

可套用题型 TOPIC LINKS

  • Favourite subject
  • A difficult thing
  • Success
  • A goal
  • An achievement
  • A challenge
  • A first experience
  • Advice you followed

参考正文 SAMPLE ANSWER

My favourite subject is science, and I never expected to love it so much.

In primary school, science was mainly about outdoor activities, and I only had a basic understanding of it.

Things changed in middle school. I met a great tutor, and we did lots of real lab experiments. But I struggled a lot at first. I wrote poor reports, got low scores and simply memorised facts without thinking.

Luckily, my tutor explained difficult ideas using simple everyday examples. I followed all his advice. Step by step, I truly understood science and made great progress.

Studying science taught me to be patient and learn from mistakes. It has completely changed the way I see the world.

考场速记 QUICK NOTES

  1. 01favourite subject: science
  2. 02primary school: outdoor fun + basic knowledge
  3. 03middle school: passionate tutor + real experiments
  4. 04struggle: poor reports + low marks + rote memorisation
  5. 05tutor: simple examples + useful advice
  6. 06progress and success
  7. 07lesson: patience + learning from mistakes

This frisbee means more to me than anything else. It always cheers me up whenever I feel upset.

地点

West Lake

可套用题型 TOPIC LINKS

  • A beautiful place
  • A city you visited
  • A quiet place
  • Hometown
  • A memorable event
  • A journey

参考正文 SAMPLE ANSWER

I’d like to talk about West Lake in Hangzhou. There is an old temple by the lake, with beautiful lotus flowers blooming everywhere in summer.

One summer, we were touring the temple when it suddenly began to rain heavily. We rushed into a tiny wonton shop nearby for shelter. An elderly couple ran the shop and warmly served us delicious hot wontons.

Years later, I returned to that spot. Sadly, the small wonton shop was gone.

This trip left me with vivid memories. The beautiful lake view and the kind elderly couple made the place unforgettable.

  2. 02summer lotus flowers
  3. 03sudden rain → shelter in wonton shop
  4. 04kind elderly couple
  5. 05return years later: shop gone
  6. 06warm, unforgettable memory

08

自然体验

A Peaceful Day by West Lake

可套用题型 TOPIC LINKS

  • A natural place
  • A park
  • Scenery
  • Weather
  • An animal
  • An environmental problem
  • A place to relax

参考正文 SAMPLE ANSWER

I’d like to talk about a memorable experience in nature by West Lake.

In summer, the lake is covered with lotus flowers. I walked along the bank and saw fish swimming in the clear water. Suddenly, a light rain began to fall, and I rested under the roof of an old temple.

The quiet scenery calmed me down completely. This trip made me realise that nature is a relaxing escape from busy city life.

However, it is truly sad that many lakes and parks are now polluted.

We should protect wild plants and keep the water clean so that beautiful views like this remain for everyone. This peaceful memory has always stayed with me.

考场速记 QUICK NOTES

  1. 01West Lake + summer lotus flowers
  2. 02fish + light rain + old temple
  3. 03peaceful; escape from city life
  4. 04environmental pollution
  5. 05protect nature + unforgettable memory

09

传统食物

Dumplings

可套用题型 TOPIC LINKS

  • Traditional food
  • A cultural event
  • A festival
  • Advice you followed
  • A school subject
  • A local improvement
  • Learning something new

参考正文 SAMPLE ANSWER

I’d like to talk about dumplings, my favourite traditional Chinese food.

We always make dumplings together during the Spring Festival. Every year, my grandma teaches me to wrap them with pork and vegetable fillings.

Making dumplings taught me a useful life lesson: patience brings good results. I used to ruin the wrappers because I worked too quickly, but I improved after following my grandma’s tips.

I believe traditional cooking should be taught at school because it helps young people understand our culture.

If more local schools held dumpling-making classes, they could bring people in the community closer together.

考场速记 QUICK NOTES

  1. 01traditional food: dumplings
  2. 02Spring Festival + make them with grandma
  3. 03grandma’s advice: be patient
  4. 04traditional cooking at school
  5. 05local improvement: dumpling-making activities
